Frequently Asked

Buyer Questions,
Plainly Answered

The questions facility managers actually ask before signing a janitorial contract. If yours isn't here, call us.

What makes Cleared to Clean different from a regular commercial janitorial company?
Our staff hold active federal security clearances. That means we can operate inside facilities — government buildings, SCIFs, contractor sites, healthcare systems with sensitive areas — that standard cleaning vendors are denied access to. We're vetted by the same standards our clients are held to.
Do you serve facilities that don't require security clearance?
Yes. We service corporate offices, medical practices, retail, fitness centers, restaurants, and condo buildings throughout Northern Virginia and Florida. The same documented procedures, vetted crews, and compliance discipline we use in cleared facilities apply to every account.
How quickly can you start service?
Most recurring accounts go live within 7 business days of contract signature. For cleared facilities or accounts requiring badge processing, we work to your timeline. Emergency or specialty cleans can often be scheduled within 24–48 hours.
What areas do you cover in Northern Virginia and Florida?
Northern Virginia: Arlington, Alexandria, Fairfax, Tysons, Reston, Falls Church, Manassas, and the wider DMV corridor. Florida: Orlando, Miami, and surrounding metro areas. Outside those zones, ask — we travel for the right accounts.
Are your cleaning products safe for medical and high-sensitivity environments?
Yes. We use EPA-certified green cleaning products as our standard. For medical, dental, and lab environments we follow infection-control protocols with EPA-registered disinfectants appropriate to the facility type. Product lists and SDS sheets are available on request.
Do you provide day porter services?
Yes. A day porter is a uniformed, on-site cleaning professional present during your operating hours — handling lobby presentation, restroom upkeep, restocking, conference-room turnovers, and ongoing high-touch surface cleaning. It's our most-requested service for Class A corporate, medical, and government facilities.
Are you licensed, bonded, and insured?
Fully licensed in Virginia and Florida, bonded, and covered with $2M general liability insurance. SAM.gov registered under NAICS 561720 (Janitorial), 561740 (Carpet & Upholstery Cleaning), and PSC code S201 (Federal Custodial). Certificates of insurance and additional-insured endorsements provided on request.
How much does commercial cleaning cost in Northern Virginia or Florida?
Pricing depends on square footage, frequency, scope (basic janitorial vs. floor care vs. day porter), and any specialty requirements like clearance, infection control, or extended hours. Most accounts fall in a predictable range we can quote within 24 hours of a quick walk-through call. We send a written proposal with no obligation.

Still have questions? Call us — we answer the phone.
